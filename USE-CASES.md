# Nucleus Hydrolyzer: Industry Applications

Section label on the site: **Use Cases**. Heading: **Industry Applications**.

> Any sector operating internal combustion engines benefits from the Nucleus — from logistics to maritime.

On the site each use case is a card. The front shows a photo with the number and name; click or tap flips it to the description below.

---

### 01. Logistics Fleet
Reduce operational fuel costs and emissions for trucks, buses, and delivery vehicles in high-mileage environments.

Photo: `assets/applications/logistics-fleet.jpg` (alt: "Logistics fleet truck on the highway")

### 02. Construction Fleet
Enhances combustion efficiency in excavators, cranes, and mixers. Reduces soot buildup and overheating.

Photo: `assets/applications/construction-fleet.jpg` (alt: "Construction fleet excavator on site")

### 03. Agriculture
Optimises combustion in tractors and harvesters. Low-maintenance and durable for rural environments.

Photo: `assets/applications/agriculture-fleet.jpg` (alt: "Agricultural tractor in a field")

### 04. Public Transport
Improves low-RPM combustion ideal for stop-and-go traffic. Visibly reduces urban black-smoke emissions.

Photo: `assets/applications/public-transport.jpg` (alt: "Public transport bus")

### 05. Government Fleet
Supports national green-fleet initiatives. Quick, low-risk deployment with no engine modifications.

Photo: `assets/applications/government-fleet.jpg` (alt: "Government fleet vehicle")

### 06. Passenger Cars
Improves mileage and reduces engine temperature for everyday drivers. No structural engine changes.

Photo: `assets/applications/passenger-cars.jpg` (alt: "Passenger car on the road")

### 07. Power Generators
Plug-and-play for stationary power, marine, and offshore installations. Prevents turbine fouling.

Photo: `assets/applications/power-generator.jpg` (alt: "Industrial power generator")

### 08. Maritime Fleet
Integrates with marine diesel engines. Aligns with IMO decarbonization targets.

Photo: `assets/applications/maritime-fleet.jpg` (alt: "Maritime vessel at sea")
